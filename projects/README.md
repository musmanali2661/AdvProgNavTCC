# Employable Project (Weeks 21–26)

Runs alongside regular classes for 6 weeks. **Alternative:** 2 weeks of on-the-job (industrial) training.

## Guidelines

- Each trainee is assigned an independent project, similar to a final-year project (FYP)
- The project is based on the trainee's aptitude and acquired skills
- It is designed around emerging trends in the local and global market
- It may be based on an entrepreneurial idea that leads to employment

## Assessment and showcase

- Final viva/assessment on the project
- Presented in skills competitions at zonal, regional and national level
- Presented to industrialists for commercialization
- The best business idea is placed in the NAVTTC business incubation center
