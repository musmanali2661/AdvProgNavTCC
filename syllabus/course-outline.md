# Course Outline

**Course title:** Artificial Intelligence (Machine Learning and Deep Learning)
**Program:** NAVTTC, Prime Minister's Hunarmand Pakistan Program ("Skills for All")
**Duration:** 26 weeks · 520 contact hours · 20% theory / 80% practical
**Learning place:** Classroom / lab · 20–24 trainees

## Objective

Employable skills and hands-on practice in Artificial Intelligence, including specialization in NLP or computer vision.

## Competencies

By the end of the course, trainees should have:

- An understanding of the core concepts of artificial intelligence and machine learning
- Knowledge of state-of-the-art machine learning techniques
- Hands-on experience with exploratory data analysis
- Practical experience with model design and evaluation
- Familiarity with scikit-learn, pandas, NumPy, TensorFlow, PyTorch and Keras

## NLP specialization outcomes

- Use TensorFlow pipelines for NLP
- Build models that extract information from textual data
- Text processing fundamentals: normalization, stemming, lemmatization
- Evaluation metrics for NLP models
- Statistical language models such as Hidden Markov Models (HMM)
- Part-of-speech (POS) tagging
- Named entity recognition
- Word embeddings and deep learning techniques for NLP
- Deploying an NLP model

## Course phases

| Phase | Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Python foundations | 1–3 | Python, control flow, data types, exceptions |
| 2. Data science toolkit | 4–6 | NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib, problem/knowledge representation |
| 3. Machine learning | 7–11 | Search, statistics, regression, classification, SVM, clustering, time series, HMM |
| 4. Neural networks | 12–13 | MLP, feed-forward networks, backpropagation, activation, loss, optimization |
| 5. NLP | 14, 16 | Linguistics, NLTK, text cleaning, vectorization, embeddings |
| Mid-term | 15 | Mid-term assignment |
| 6. Computer vision | 17–18 | OpenCV basics, image operations, thresholding, mask detection |
| 7. Deep learning | 19–20, 22–25 | Deep learning, TensorFlow, CNNs, colorization, chatbot, TTS |
| 8. Employable project | 21–26 | Independent project (or 2 weeks on-the-job training) |
| 9. Entrepreneurship | 26 | CV, freelancing, business fundamentals, final assessment |

## Assessment

- **Mid-term assignment:** week 15 ([assessments/midterm/](../assessments/midterm/))
- **Employable project:** weeks 21–26, final viva, presented at skills competitions ([projects/](../projects/))
- **Final assessment:** week 26 ([assessments/final/](../assessments/final/))

## Job roles

AI Associate Engineer · Machine Learning Associate Analyst · Assistant Data Analyst · Research Assistant

Companies listed in the curriculum: Careem, Afiniti, Addo.ai, Arbisoft, i2c, Xavor, Fiverivers Technologies,
Confiz, Crossover, NetSol, research institutes, and private institutes with an ML department.

## Instructor qualification

At minimum, a bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering or Electrical Engineering (Hons), plus 3 years of development experience in the trade.
